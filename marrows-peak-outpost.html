Marrows Peak Outpost

Slowertrain August 5, 2026

Marrow's Peak Outpost is the only outpost you can find in the Devil's Roar. I do recall a time where I experienced an eruption on the island, but that was years ago and I have not experienced it since I have resumed playing. These days, you only have to fear the geysers and other players.

During our last trip, after unloading our loot, I decided to check in on the local space program. For those unfamiliar with it, you can wedge a sloop (I have only tested with a sloop) between the island and the small rock off the north side of the dock to the sovereign. If you get your ship in there and then use the harpoon to pull the front of the ship around the rock, towards the sovereign (use full-sails if you want a little extra push), the game engine may decide that your ship belongs in the sky. The results are pretty inconsistent but, if you only get a small launch, it is very easy to keep attempting until you are rewarded with liftoff. In our case, after about half a dozen tries, we eventually were shot up and came down on the island.

To the developers, please do not fix this. The setup takes enough time that it is not strategically useful and the launch can throw crew members, who are not holding onto something, off of the ship.

Above is the picture of our last attempt. I knew there was a chance to land on the island but that was not my goal. There was another player sailing nearby and I wanted to give them a show. I am unsure how much they saw, but they definitely saw the end result. While I was snapping the first two pictures, said player snuck onto the island and attempted to sink our ship with a powder keg of some kind. I did manage to catch the beginning of the explosion in my third picture below.

The initial explosion killed my friend on the ship but my distance kept me safe. I was able to revive my friend but she was killed again as our Cannons of Rage chain-reacted from the first explosion. After a second revive, we spotted the player and dispatched them. Fortunately, our park-job spared our ship from having the sea pour in our damaged hull. As we patched up our ship, we also discovered that the explosion had pushed our ship close enough to the beach where we could use the harpoon to pull us back out to sea.
